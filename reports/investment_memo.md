This directly contradicts the claim that a single customer pays $15K per month. Alternatively, if each customer truly pays $180K ARR annually, 8 customers should generate **$\$1,440,000\text{ ARR}$**, not $120K. This fundamental accounting error undermines the reliability of management's financial reporting.
